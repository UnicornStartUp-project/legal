Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-27

This policy describes the personal automation tool Kokoro Shotengai Pipeline (Japanese_Senior_Video_pipeline), operated solely by the owner of the YouTube channel こころの縁側.

1. Scope

This is a private, single-operator tool. It is not offered to the public and does not have third-party end users. It accesses only the operator's own Google/YouTube account data.

2. Data accessed (YouTube API Services)

  • youtube.readonly — read the operator's own channel and video information.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — read the operator's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, audience, etc.).
  • youtube.upload — upload the operator's own videos to the operator's own channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl — manage the operator's own videos, captions, playlists and comments.

3. How data is used and stored

Retrieved data is used only to produce and improve the operator's own videos. It is stored locally on the operator's own computer (SQLite/JSON). It is not sold, shared, transferred, or disclosed to any third party.

4. Third parties

No data is shared with third parties. The tool does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

5. Retention and deletion

Locally stored data can be deleted at any time by the operator. OAuth access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. YouTube / Google policies

Use of information received from YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
